## How it is tested

- **Split:** No time split: nothing is trained; every document was read after the prompt was frozen, and none changed it.
- **Baseline:** One AI read taken as it answers, with no second read and no check (what ml/nlp_extractor.py did before PLAN.md 6.2).
- **Headline:** `planted_missed_share` on the `evaluation` set. A group "beats the baseline" only when the model is strictly better there; under 5 cases it has "not enough data" and the app should show no estimate.
- **Regions:** S&P's (the validation report's): United States, Europe, emerging markets, other developed.
- **Bar:** Set before any document was read: no wrong figure or label and no planted misread left unflagged, and at least 80% of the truth file's figures found without amber; production reads documents only while the headline set meets it.

Statistics:

- `planted_missed_share` (lower is better; CI fails a change that makes it worse by more than 0): Share of misreads planted in read A (a digit changed in the value and its quote, one at a time) still shown without amber.
- `truth_missed` (lower is better; CI fails a change that makes it worse by more than 0): Figures and labels of the truth file shown without amber and not as the document prints them.
- `truth_found_share` (higher is better; CI fails a change that makes it worse by more than 0.03): Share of the truth file's figures shown without amber, as the document prints them.
- `labels_right_share` (higher is better; CI fails a change that makes it worse by more than 0): Share of currency, unit, standard, fiscal year end and scope read right without amber.
- `amber_share` (lower is better; CI fails a change that makes it worse by more than 0.03): Share of values shown amber (the person must look at each one).

## Limitations

- Eight documents: four German-language (three from Germany, one from Austria), one each in Japanese, French, Spanish and English (India); no US filer, no information pack (they are not public), no scan.
- The truth file holds each document's headline figures only (four to nine of them); a wrong figure outside it is seen only through the planted misreads and the quote check.
- The quote check needs a text layer: on a scanned page a quote can't be looked up, and two reads making the same mistake there would pass.
- Both reads may take the same line for a figure the document prints twice (an operating result before and after other items, profit before and after minorities): the truth file allows either.
