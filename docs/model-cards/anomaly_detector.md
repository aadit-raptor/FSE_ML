## How it is tested

- **Split:** Walk-forward by year the deal closed: trained on cases before each cutoff (2008, 2010), tested on the cases from it to the next.
- **Baseline:** Debt / EBITDA as the score; flagged above 6x (the supervisory leverage limit the deal's risk warnings use).
- **Headline:** `auc` on the `out_of_time` set. A group "beats the baseline" only when the model is strictly better there; under 5 cases it has "not enough data" and the app should show no estimate.
- **Regions:** S&P's (the validation report's): United States, Europe, emerging markets, other developed.

Statistics:

- `auc` (higher is better; CI fails a change that makes it worse by more than 0.005): Chance a distressed deal scores above a deal that wasn't (0.5 = no better than chance).
- `distress_flagged` (higher is better; CI fails a change that makes it worse by more than 0.005): Share of distressed deals flagged.
- `false_alarms` (lower is better; CI fails a change that makes it worse by more than 0.005): Share of deals without distress flagged.

## Limitations

- Every training deal is a US buyout: Europe, emerging markets and other developed markets have no cases, so the card shows not enough data there.
- The deal figures were typed in without sources; PLAN.md 5.2 rebuilds the score on sourced, regional market data.
- Fourteen out-of-time cases, four of them distressed: each case moves the statistics by several points.
- The score adds fixed rule-of-thumb terms (leverage, multiple, coverage) to the forest's anomaly severity; the card evaluates the score as the app shows it.
