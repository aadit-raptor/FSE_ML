## How it is tested

- **Split:** No time split: nothing is trained; the held-out set is filings published or read after the prompt and the checks were frozen, and the development set is labelled in-sample.
- **Baseline:** Read A alone, with the sums the table states checked (what one AI read and arithmetic give).
- **Headline:** `planted_missed_share` on the `held_out` set. A group "beats the baseline" only when the model is strictly better there; under 5 cases it has "not enough data" and the app should show no estimate.
- **Regions:** India only for now: every filing is an Indian listed company's quarterly results.
- **Bar:** No wrong figure left unflagged and under 3% of figures amber (R0's 2%, raised by the user on 2026-10-16: PLAN.md R1b); production reads filings only while the headline set meets it.

Statistics:

- `planted_missed_share` (lower is better; CI fails a change that makes it worse by more than 0): Share of wrong digits planted in read A (one at a time) that the reader still shows without amber.
- `amber_share` (lower is better; CI fails a change that makes it worse by more than 0.002): Share of figures shown amber (the analyst must look at each one).
- `truth_missed` (lower is better; CI fails a change that makes it worse by more than 0): Figures of the truth file (BSE's own, where saved) shown without amber and not as printed.
- `truth_confirmed_share` (higher is better; CI fails a change that makes it worse by more than 0.02): Share of the truth file's figures the reader shows, without amber, as BSE gives them.

## Limitations

- Two reads making the same mistake in a figure outside every sum is not seen: the planted digits test one read's misreads, and the truth file covers the quarter's headline figures of 17 filings only (ten development, seven held-out).
- The development set's prompt and checks were worked out on those very filings; only the held-out set says how the reader does on new ones.
- Six development filings were read by R0's first pass, the results table only; one (Yatharth Hospital) has a lite model's read A.
- Indian listed companies' results only; banks, insurers and lenders are left out.
- The held-out filings are eight June 2026 quarter or March 2026 year-end filings saved by hand, not a random sample; one hard scan (Larsen & Toubro's, its segment table read differently by the two models) holds most of their amber figures.
- The high-resolution trial (held_out_high) has fewer filings than the card needs: the free allowance ran out on the first day.
- A wrong year in every column heading is a period problem, not an amber figure: the analyst names the quarter, or the reads must agree on it.
